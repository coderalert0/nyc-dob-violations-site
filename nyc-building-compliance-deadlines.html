NYC building compliance deadlines

Checked against city sources on November 4, 2026

Short answer

The next major NYC building deadlines are December 31, 2026 for boiler inspections, elevator periodic and Category 1 tests, gas piping in Community Districts 4, 6, 8, 9 and 16, and Staten Island retaining walls. Bed bug reports are due December 1 to 31, and sub-cycle 10A facade reports are due February 21, 2027.

Frequently asked questions

What is the most common NYC building deadline?

December 31. Boiler inspections and elevator periodic and Category 1 tests all run on a January 1 to December 31 cycle, and gas piping, parking and retaining wall deadlines also fall on December 31 of their assigned year.

How do I know which sub-cycle my building is in?

It depends on the requirement. Facades use the last digit of the tax block number. Gas piping uses the Community District. Parking structures use the borough and Community District. Retaining walls use the borough.

Does this calendar replace official city notices?

No. It is an independent summary, checked against city sources on the date shown on this page. The city can change a rule or extend a deadline, so confirm with the source linked in each guide before you file.
