How to check NYC DOB violations by address

Checked against city sources on November 4, 2026

Short answer

You can check NYC building violations for free through the Buildings Information System (BIS) and DOB NOW Public Portal for DOB records, HPD Online for housing violations, and the OATH summons finder for ECB summonses. Each searches by address or Building Identification Number (BIN), and each covers a different agency, so check them together.

Where to look

Record type Where to search
DOB violations and complaints Buildings Information System (BIS)
DOB NOW filings and safety records DOB NOW Public Portal
HPD housing violations and complaints HPD Online
OATH and ECB summonses OATH summons finder

What the classes mean

HPD violations and DOB summonses use different scales.

Class Meaning Correction
HPD Class A Non-hazardous 90 days from service
HPD Class B Hazardous 30 days from service
HPD Class C Immediately hazardous 24 hours for most conditions
DOB Class 1 summons Immediately hazardous Correct immediately and file a Certificate of Correction

Some HPD conditions have their own time limits, and DOB and OATH notices state the exact requirements for each case. Follow the violation notice itself.

Frequently asked questions

How do I look up violations for an NYC building?

Search the address in BIS for DOB records, HPD Online for housing records and the OATH summons finder for ECB summonses. All are free.

What is a BIN?

A Building Identification Number. It identifies one building, and it is more precise than an address when a lot has several buildings.

Why do DOB and HPD show different violations?

They are separate agencies enforcing different codes. A building can have open records with one and none with the other, so check both.

Is there a way to get alerts for new violations?

The free NYC DOB Violations iPhone app checks the city's open data for your buildings and notifies you about new violations, complaints and approaching deadlines.
