## What sets the expiration date

The Department of Buildings says permits issued in DOB NOW: Build expire on the earliest of:

| Date | What happens if it comes first |
|---|---|
| An insurance policy expires (general liability, disability or workers' compensation) | Renewed before that date: the permit is extended automatically at no cost. Lapsed: the permit has to be renewed, with the $130 fee |
| The license expires | The same |
| One year from the date of issuance | The permit has to be renewed, with the $130 fee |

So a permit can expire months before its year is up, because of a contractor's insurance certificate the owner never sees. DOB says the new date appears in DOB NOW at the end of the day of the old expiration date.

Permits are renewed in the system they were issued in: DOB NOW: Build, or eFiling and BIS for older jobs. For BIS jobs, updating insurance or license dates extends permits due to expire in the next 31 days, overnight.

## What working on an expired permit costs

Under 1 RCNY §102-04:

| Violation | One- or two-family home | Other buildings |
|---|---|---|
| Work after the permit expired, or after hours without a variance | $600 | $6,000 |
| Work with no permit | 6 times the permit fee, at least $600 and at most $10,000 | 21 times the permit fee, at least $6,000 and at most $15,000 |

An apartment inside a co-op or condo counts as one- or two-family; work in common areas doesn't. The penalty for unpermitted work has to be paid before DOB will renew the permit. These are DOB civil penalties; a summons heard at OATH can add its own.

## How to check a building's permits

| Where | What it has |
|---|---|
| [DOB NOW Public Portal](https://a810-dobnow.nyc.gov/publish/Index.html) | Jobs and permits filed in DOB NOW |
| [Buildings Information System (BIS)](https://a810-bisweb.nyc.gov/bisweb/bispi00.jsp) | Older jobs and permits |
| [NYC Open Data: DOB NOW approved permits](https://data.cityofnewyork.us/Housing-Development/DOB-NOW-Build-Approved-Permits/rbx6-tga4) | DOB NOW permits as data |
| [NYC Open Data: DOB permit issuance](https://data.cityofnewyork.us/Housing-Development/DOB-Permit-Issuance/ipu4-2q9a) | BIS permits as data |

1. Search the address in both DOB NOW and BIS: a building with long-running work often has permits in each.
2. Look at the expiration date on each permit, not on the job.
3. A renewed permit can appear more than once. Use the latest date.
4. Match the work going on to a permit for that work type. A plumbing permit doesn't cover construction.
5. Ask the contractor when their insurance expires, and note the earlier date.

## In the app

NYC DOB Violations shows each building's permits from DOB NOW and BIS, soonest to expire first, and alerts you 30 and 7 days before one expires. It is free for one building.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long is a DOB work permit good for?

Up to one year from issuance, and less if the contractor's insurance or license expires first.

### How much does it cost to renew?

$130 when insurance or the license lapsed, or when more than a year has passed since issuance. Renewing the insurance or license before it expires extends a DOB NOW permit at no cost.

### Can a contractor keep working while the renewal is pending?

The rule penalizes work performed after the permit expired, and the DOB pages cited here give no exception for a renewal that has been filed but not issued. Ask DOB before relying on one.
